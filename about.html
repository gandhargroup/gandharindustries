Who We Are

Gandhar Group's Legacy of Powering India's Growth

Established in 1972, Gandhar Group has emerged as Asia's leading manufacturer and supplier of high-quality Pre-Stressed Concrete (PSC) and Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) poles. Over the decades, the company has played a significant role in India's electrification journey, supporting large-scale infrastructure and energy projects across the country. With a strong focus on quality and reliability, Gandhar Group has built a reputation as a trusted partner in the power and infrastructure sectors.

With an impressive annual production capacity of 3 million poles and 17 strategically located manufacturing facilities across Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Bihar, Odisha, Kerala and Madhya Pradesh, Gandhar Group is well-equipped to meet the growing demands of infrastructure development in India and beyond. Its diverse product portfolio includes PSC, RCC, and spun poles, along with precast components such as base plates and cube ballast, all designed to deliver durability, performance, and consistency at scale.

Building on its strong foundation, Gandhar Group has expanded into the precast concrete segment, integrating advanced technologies to enable faster execution, superior quality control, and cost-efficient construction. Serving sectors like power, railways, and urban infrastructure, the company continues to strengthen its global footprint. Backed by operational excellence and a commitment to innovation, Gandhar Group remains focused on shaping the future of infrastructure while delivering sustainable and scalable solutions worldwide.

Restoring Connectivity When It Matters Most

Leading the Way in Disaster Recovery

Gujarat Earthquake (2001) – Supplied critical PSC poles for rebuilding efforts in Gujarat, demonstrating reliability during major emergencies.
Thane Cyclone (2011) – Fulfilled Tamil Nadu's entire requirement of around 70,000 PSC poles, earning recognition from the Tamil Nadu Chief Minister for swift and effective disaster response.
HudHud Cyclone (2014) – Played a crucial role in restoring power infrastructure in Visakhapatnam and surrounding areas by supplying 25,000 PSC poles in partnership with the Andhra Pradesh government.
Gaja Cyclone (2018) – Supplied approximately 2,00,000 poles within 21 days to aid power recovery efforts in Tamil Nadu's coastal districts.
From a Humble Beginning to Industry Leader

The Gandhar Group Story

1972 – 2000

The Early Years

Gandhar Group's journey began in 1972 with a clear vision to contribute to India's growing power infrastructure needs. Starting with a single manufacturing unit focused on producing high-quality PSC poles, the company's early years were defined by a relentless commitment to craftsmanship, integrity, and serving local communities.

Early 2000s

Pioneering Growth in Rajasthan

As the demand for electrification surged across India, Gandhar Group launched three new plants in Rajasthan in 2009, supporting the state's electrification projects including those near the Indian border, strengthening border energy security.

2011

Geographic Expansion

Gandhar Group expanded beyond Rajasthan by establishing its first manufacturing facility in Mandideep, Madhya Pradesh — a strategic move towards geographic diversification and broader market reach.

2012 – 2013

Revolutionary Transformation

Two manufacturing plants launched in Tamil Nadu, setting new industry benchmarks. Since then the Group has supplied the majority of Tamil Nadu's concrete pole requirements and is TNEB's most trusted partner during natural calamities.

2014 – 2017

Unstoppable Growth

Three plants established in Bihar, becoming the largest supplier in the state. In 2016 a facility in Odisha was launched, meeting electrification needs and supplying poles across the entire state during disaster relief.

2018 – 2025

Gandhar Group's Power Surge

Unveiled the world's largest PSC and RCC pole manufacturing plant serving Tamil Nadu and Kerala, increased Tamil Nadu capacity in 2024, and expanded into Bihar in early 2025.

2025 – Present

Expanding Horizons

Took over a new manufacturing plant in Kerala, entered the precast industry in Odisha in early 2026, and established an additional precast facility in Tamil Nadu later in 2026.

Looking Ahead

Fueling the Future with Unstoppable Force

As India accelerates toward universal electrification and next-generation infrastructure, Gandhar Group is scaling new heights with 17 manufacturing plants nationwide. In 2026, the Group made a bold strategic leap into the precast concrete industry, establishing advanced facilities in Odisha and Tamil Nadu to redefine speed, quality, and efficiency in construction.

Precast is not just an expansion—it is a transformation. By enabling rapid execution, superior consistency, and cost optimization, Gandhar Group is positioning itself at the forefront of infrastructure innovation across power, railways, and urban development. Driven by decades of expertise, a future-ready workforce, and an unwavering commitment to innovation and sustainability, the Group is set to emerge as a dominant force in both domestic and global infrastructure landscapes.
